Mapp PWA application
********************

This app is designed to enable you to plan and share sampling points in the 
field. It can generate sampling points based on specified criteria, and you 
can save these and share them with other users or with other apps.

This is an experimental app; if you’re interested, please get in touch with the 
developers.

See in action `here <https://ebiok.science.unideb.hu/mapp/index.html>`_.
